Model and method

The device is N nanowires of length L connecting two bulk electrodes, at positions x1 < … < xN measured in units of the outer separation W, with x1 = −½ and xN = +½. Loop k lies between wires k and k+1 and holds nk vortices; b = BLW/Φ₀.

Meissner phase equation. \(\varphi_{k+1}-\varphi_k = 2\pi b\,(x_{k+1}-x_k) - 2\pi n_k\), so \(\varphi_i=\varphi_1+2\pi b(x_i-x_1)-2\pi\sum_{k=1}^{i-1}n_k\).

Characteristic phase. \(\varphi=\frac1N\sum_i\varphi_i\) gives \(\varphi_i=\varphi+a_i+c_i\) with

\[a_i = 2\pi b\Big(x_i-\frac1N\sum_{k=1}^N x_k\Big),\qquad c_i = 2\pi\Big(\frac1N\sum_{p=1}^{N-1}(N-p)\,n_p-\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}n_j\Big).\]

CPR. Likharev: \(I_i/I_0=\varphi_i/\varphi_0-(\varphi_i/\varphi_0)^3\). Summing over wires and using \(\sum_i a_i=\sum_i c_i=0\):

\[\frac{I}{I_0}=A\varphi-D\varphi^3-R,\quad A=\frac{N\varphi_0^2-3\sum_i(a_i+c_i)^2}{\varphi_0^3},\quad D=\frac{N}{\varphi_0^3},\quad R=\frac{\sum_i(a_i+c_i)^3}{\varphi_0^3}.\]

For the 2m+1 CPR, \(I_i/I_0=\varphi_i/\varphi_0-a(\varphi_i/\varphi_0)^{2m+1}\), the explorer evaluates the summed CPR and its integral wire by wire (this is exact; it equals the binomial expansion in the notes term by term).

Hamiltonian. \(\hat H=-4E_C\,\partial_\varphi^2+\frac{\hbar I_0}{2e}U(\varphi)\), with \(U=\tfrac12A\varphi^2-\tfrac14D\varphi^4-R\varphi\) for the Likharev CPR.

Numerics. The page runs the same steps as N_Nanowire_Potential_Plots.py (ai, ci, the ten sums, A, D, R, stationary points of U). Energy levels: second-order finite differences on grids of 1500 and 3000 points spanning the well up to about 25ħω above the bottom (or up to the barrier tops if they are lower), eigenvalues by Sturm-sequence bisection, then Richardson extrapolation to remove the O(dx²) grid error. For the symmetric case the anharmonicity agrees with first-order perturbation theory, α ≈ −6DEC/A. On load, the page checks itself against the closed forms for N = 2 and N = 3 (see “Wire-by-wire numbers”).

References: C. Sun and A. Bezryadin, Dayem loop qubit, arXiv:2603.17214; C. Sun et al., Nano Express 6, 035014 (2025).
